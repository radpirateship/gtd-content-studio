Our take

Why we carry OxyRevo

We stock four hyperbaric brands and OxyRevo is the one we recommend most often to first-time buyers. The reason is unglamorous: the zippers, the compressor and the parts pipeline. A soft-shell chamber lives or dies on its zipper track, and OxyRevo's dual-track TIZIP design has given us the lowest warranty-claim rate of anything we sell in the category.

They also ship a real oxygen concentrator with every chamber rather than selling it as an upsell, and their US warehouse stocks compressors, filters and liners — so a repair is a week, not a quarter.

Heritage

Questions

OxyRevo FAQ

How does OxyRevo compare to the other brands you carry?–
OxyRevo sits in the middle on price and at the top on serviceability. If you want the quietest possible compressor, look at our premium line; if you want a chamber that will still have parts available in 2032, this is the one we point people to.
Is the oxygen concentrator really included?+
Yes — a 10 LPM concentrator ships with every soft-shell chamber. Hard-shell models are quoted with a clinic-spec concentrator sized to the chamber volume.
What happens if a zipper or compressor fails?+
Call or email us — we file the claim and ship the part from US stock, typically within a week. For zipper track damage we send a replacement envelope rather than attempting a field repair.
